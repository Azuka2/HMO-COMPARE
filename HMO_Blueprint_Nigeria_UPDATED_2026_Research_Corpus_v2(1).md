# 0. CRITICAL CORRECTION TO EARLIER RESEARCH

The earlier research corpus contained older premium figures for several HMOs.

That is not acceptable for a production HMO comparison engine.

This version therefore adopts:

> **CURRENT-DATE-FIRST PRICING**

A premium is not “current” merely because it appeared on a web page.

Every price must now carry:

- `price_amount`
- `currency`
- `price_type`
- `plan_name`
- `customer_type`
- `location`
- `lives`
- `source`
- `source_date`
- `retrieved_date`
- `effective_date` if stated
- `verification_status`
- `confidence`

### Price types

1. CURRENT_OFFICIAL_PUBLISHED
2. CURRENT_OFFICIAL_QUOTED
3. CURRENT_OBSERVED
4. NEGOTIATED_CUSTOM
5. PROMOTIONAL
6. HISTORICAL
7. MARKET_REPORTED
8. ESTIMATED
9. UNKNOWN

Never use an older published price simply because the newer figure cannot be found.

Instead say:

> **Current price not publicly verified.**
