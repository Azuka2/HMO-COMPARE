## **2\. NHIA Regulatory Documents & Guidelines**

### **NHIA Operational Guidelines (Revised)** 

Date Launched: July 2026  
Key Points:

* Purpose: Clarify roles of all stakeholders in Nigeria's health insurance ecosystem  
* Basic Minimum Package: Specifies minimum health services for all Nigerians  
* Accreditation Standards: Sets standards for accrediting critical stakeholders  
* Penalties: Defines penalties for offences committed by defaulting stakeholders  
* Key Quote from DG NHIA Prof. Nasir Sambo: "One of the cornerstones of these guidelines is the principle of equity. We firmly believe that every citizen of this great Nation has the right to healthcare services that are not only accessible but also affordable."

How to access: The official document is available at NHIA Headquarters, Abuja. For the full PDF, visit the NHIA official website at [nhia.gov.ng](https://nhia.gov.ng/)

### **NHIA Act 2022 \- Key Changes** 

* Replaced: The old NHIS Act 2004  
* Made health insurance mandatory for all Nigerians and legal residents  
* State Health Insurance Schemes (SHIS): HMOs no longer have direct fund management authority (which sits with SHIS)  
* HMO role: May collect premiums on behalf of SHIS where appointed, but pooled funds must be remitted to SHIS for management  
* Digital technology embedded as core infrastructure

### **NHIA Enrollment Requirements for Employers** 

Steps for Enrollment:

1. Register with NHIA (7-14 days): CAC registration, TIN, employee list with NINs, bank details  
2. Choose HMO partner (e.g., Hygeia, AXA Mansard, Reliance Health, Avon, Leadway Health)  
3. Enroll employees (14-21 days): Forms, passport photos, NIN, employment letters  
4. Set up monthly payments (within 30 days of salary payment)

Critical Requirement: Valid National Identification Number (NIN) is a hard requirement for NHIA and HMO enrollment

| HMO Tier | Minimum Capital | Asset Composition |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |

Application Requirements:

1. Corporate Registration: Certificate of Incorporation from CAC  
2. Memorandum & Articles: Must allow HMO operations  
3. Advance Payment Guarantee: Security Deposit (Bond) of ₦50,000,000 or 1% of projected annual turnover  
4. Operational Infrastructure: Zone-level offices for national HMOs  
5. Quality Assurance System: Documented QA protocols and quarterly facility assessments  
6. Bank Accounts: Separate capitation, fee-for-service, and administration-charge accounts

Fee Structure:

| Fee Type | Amount (₦) | Validity/Notes |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |

| Renewal Fee | 200,000 | Every two years |
| Security Deposit (Bond) | 50,000,000 | Advance Payment Guarantee |
| Total Upfront Cost | 50,800,000 | Excludes share capital |

7-Stage Licensing Process:

1. Document Preparation: CAC papers, Board resolution, audited accounts, bond certificate  
2. Fee Payment: Remita payment of application and accreditation fees  
3. Submission: Deliver hard and electronic copies to Accreditation Division, Abuja  
4. Preliminary Screening: NHIA checks completeness within 14 days  
5. Site Inspection: NHIA team visits offices and reviews QA systems  
6. Committee Review: Monthly meeting of Accreditation Committee  
7. License Issuance: 2-year Certificate of Accreditation

Additional Requirements:

* Board of Directors details

# **HMO Value Chain & Strategic Advantages Analysis**

## **Research Date: August 26, 2026**

### **Executive Summary**

This document examines the strategic value chain advantages of Nigeria's major HMOs. A value chain advantage exists when an HMO's parent company, sister companies, or strategic partners provide it with leverage that competitors lack — such as in-house pharmaceuticals, diagnostics, or deep technology integration. These advantages can translate into cost savings, faster service delivery, and improved customer experience.

---

## **1\. Clearline HMO – The "Integrated Healthcare Ecosystem" Model**

Parent Company & Value Chain Structure:

| Entity | Role in Value Chain |
| :---- | :---- |
| Clearline HMO | Health insurance provider |
| Greenlife Pharmaceuticals Ltd | Drug manufacturing & supply (parent/sister company) |
| Clinix Healthcare | Diagnostic center subsidiary |

Advantage Analysis:

Clearline operates a vertically integrated healthcare model, leveraging its parent/sister companies to control critical parts of the healthcare delivery chain.

Pharmaceutical Advantage (Greenlife):

* Greenlife Pharmaceuticals is a drug manufacturer (they produce Leonard DS, among other medications)  
* Clearline HMO partnered with Greenlife for joint CSR events like World Malaria Day initiatives to supply drugs to individuals who test positive for malaria   
* This creates a closed-loop pharmaceutical supply chain — Clearline's members have access to drugs produced within the group, potentially reducing costs and ensuring availability 

Diagnostic Advantage (Clinix):

* Clinix Healthcare operates as a diagnostic center within the ecosystem  
* During the Makoko malaria outreach, Clinix provided diagnostic testing while Clearline and Greenlife provided the insurance and drugs, respectively   
* This means Clearline can potentially control diagnostic quality and turnaround times by using Clinix facilities

Strategic Impact:

* Cost Efficiency: Potential for internal transfer pricing and reduced drug procurement costs  
* Quality Control: Direct oversight of pharmaceutical and diagnostic quality  
* Fast Service: Immediate access to diagnostic centers and drug supply  
* CSR/Outreach: Ability to execute comprehensive health campaigns (test \+ treat \+ insure)

### **NHIA Operational Guidelines (2023/2026)** 

The Federal Government launched the NHIA Operational Guidelines to ensure Universal Health Coverage and Compulsory Health Insurance. The guidelines specify the "basic minimum package of health services for all Nigerians" .

Key NHIA Coverage Details:

* Basic Minimum Package of Health Services (BMPHS): A set of preventive, protective, promotive, curative, and rehabilitative health services to be developed and reviewed by the Minister of Health .  
* Enrollment Requirement: Complete payment of the applicable premium (for GIFSHIP: ₦38,718 per person) .
